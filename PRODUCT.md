# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4, motion (Framer Motion), Lenis, lucide-react. User-specified.

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers and engineering leads evaluating Rindrit Telaku for data engineering, AI engineering or full-stack roles. They arrive from a CV link, LinkedIn or GitHub, scan for a minute, and decide whether to open a project or send an email.

## Product Purpose

A single-page personal portfolio that makes the visitor want to see what Rindrit has built. Success: the visitor opens a project demo or GitHub repo, or writes to the email address.

## Positioning

The work is demonstrated through real system diagrams (pipeline topologies of the actual projects) and live demos, not through claims, skill bars or stat counters.

## Operating Context

Visitors read on laptops and phones. Projects link to GitHub repositories and to static HTML live demos hosted alongside the site. The CV is a one-page PDF.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single route `/` with anchored sections: work, experience, about, contact.
- Three real projects: FovIQ (EXIF analytics ETL), Transit Lens (GTFS delay dashboard), RAG Knowledge System (local retrieval augmented generation).
- One real role: ProQu, Junior Ingestion Specialist, May 2026 to present, remote.
- No CMS; all copy lives in `content/site.ts`.
- No Three.js, no GSAP (user decision).

## Brand Commitments

- Name-first opening: the first viewport shows only the name.
- Dark-first, near-black and charcoal, off-white type, one accent (icy cyan, user decision), selective translucent glass surfaces, persistent floating bottom navigation.
- No hacker aesthetics: no fake terminal, no matrix rain, no green code, no AI brain imagery. No skill bars, no testimonials, no biography.
- Visual references named by the user: ipsitabajpai.com (name-first opening), dousanmiao.com dark mode (glass, floating nav, atmosphere). Principles only, no copying.

## Evidence on Hand

- Project screenshots: `public/projects/exif.png`, `public/projects/transit.jpg`, `public/projects/rag.png` (from the previous site).
- Live demos: `public/demos/foviq.html`, `public/demos/transit-lens.html`, `public/demos/rag.html`.
- CV: `public/RindritTelakuCV.pdf`.
- Links: github.com/RindTel, linkedin.com/in/rindrittelaku, rindritelaku@gmail.com.
- No testimonials, no metrics, no client logos. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

- Confidence through restraint: fewer elements, larger and better.
- Engineering character comes from real work: diagrams, metadata, topology.
- Every interaction must be explainable in one sentence and stay fast on phones.
- Real content only; anything missing stays missing rather than invented.
