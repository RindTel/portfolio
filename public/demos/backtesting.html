Backtest

A trading rule replayed over Bitcoin daily prices, 2020 to 2024, one day at a time with $10,000 to start. Every fill happens at the next day's open and pays a fee, half the spread and market impact. The grey dashed line is buy & hold, the bar any strategy has to clear.
